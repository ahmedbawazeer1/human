## Who to talk to

Local plant stores, gardening clubs, and online forums for houseplant enthusiasts. Approach members of Facebook groups like 'Houseplant Lovers' or 'Indoor Gardening Enthusiasts', local community centers, and botanical gardens.

## Questions (about past behaviour, never pitching)

1. What are some daily routines you have for caring for your houseplants?
2. How do you currently stay motivated to take care of your plants when you're busy?
3. Have you ever tried using a plant as a reminder or motivator in your life?
4. What do you do when you forget to water your plants and feel guilty about it?
5. Do you have any rituals or habits that help you maintain your indoor garden despite a hectic schedule?
6. How do you handle the emotional aspect of nurturing a plant, especially when it starts to show signs of stress?
7. Have you noticed any patterns in how you interact with your plants over time?
8. What are some things you've done in the past to keep your plants alive and thriving?

## Signals to listen for

- Descriptions of specific routines or habits related to plant care
- References to emotional connections or motivations tied to plants
- Talk about past struggles with maintaining plants and how they were overcome
- Mentions of guilt, responsibility, or emotional attachment to plants
- Examples of rituals, habits, or strategies used to stay consistent with plant care

## Commitments to ask for

- To share a specific routine or habit they use for caring for their plants
- To agree to a follow-up call to discuss their experiences further
- To try an early version of the app if it's developed
