ScopeGuard Toolkit - A Digital Tool to Stop Scope Creep

A toolkit designed for solo freelance web developers and small agencies to manage project scope effectively.

This product is not built yet. Join the waitlist to hear when an early version is ready. No payment is taken.

What you get

  • Helps identify and limit scope creep during project execution
  • Provides a structured process for handling change requests
  • Includes email templates to communicate project boundaries with clients

How it works

  1. Complete a scoping questionnaire at the start of a project
  2. Use a change-request process to evaluate and approve additional features
  3. Send client email templates to reinforce project boundaries and expectations

Questions

Is the ScopeGuard Toolkit available for immediate use?
No, the ScopeGuard Toolkit is currently under development and not yet available. Join the waitlist to be notified when it's ready.
How does the change-request process work?
The change-request process helps evaluate whether additional features can be added without exceeding the project budget or timeline. It requires approval from both the developer and the client.
Can I use the email templates for any type of project?
The email templates are designed for projects involving small businesses. They can be adapted for other types of projects, but they are specifically tailored for the needs of freelance web developers and small agencies.
