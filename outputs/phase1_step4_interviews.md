## Who to talk to

Local freelance web developers and small agencies (1-3 people) who build websites for small businesses. Target venues include local co-working spaces, online forums like Reddit’s r/webdev, LinkedIn groups for freelancers and agencies, and through referrals from existing clients or industry events.

## Questions (about past behaviour, never pitching)

1. What is your process for managing client expectations before starting a website project?
2. Have you ever had a situation where scope creep caused delays or budget overruns? If so, how did you handle it?
3. How do you typically track changes requested by the client during development?
4. What happens if a client wants to add new features after the initial design is approved?
5. Can you describe a time when a project became more complex than initially anticipated?
6. Have you used any tools or methods to keep projects on track and within budget?
7. How do you handle situations where the client’s needs change after the project has started?
8. What are your biggest challenges when working with small businesses that have limited technical knowledge?

## Signals to listen for

- Descriptions of past scope creep incidents
- Mentions of specific tools or methods used to manage projects
- Examples of how clients’ needs changed during development
- Comments on communication challenges with clients
- References to budget overruns or delays caused by scope changes

## Commitments to ask for

- A follow-up call to discuss a specific past experience with scope creep
- Trial use of an early version of the toolkit
- Sharing a project plan or timeline for review
