# Founder brief

- Idea: A toolkit that helps freelance web developers stop scope creep: a scoping questionnaire, a change-request process and ready-to-use client email templates.
- Skills: 6 years as a freelance web developer; writes clear documentation; basic design skills.
- Budget: $200 total.
- Time: 10 hours per week for the next 8 weeks.
- Target market: Solo freelance web developers and very small agencies (1-3 people) who build websites for small businesses.
- Values: Honesty, no hype, respect for clients' and customers' privacy, keep it affordable.
